One thing to align before launch

The reply time. The ads say « première réponse sous 24 h » (“first reply within 24 h”), your words from post 18, and your home page says « Réponse sous 24 heures ».

The Diagnostic page the ads open, and its thank-you screen, still say « premier retour sous trois jours ouvrés » (“first reply within three working days”).

Could your team change the Diagnostic page to 24 h, so the ad and the page say the same thing? Someone who clicks an ad expects the page to keep the same promise, and a same-day reply is what turns a request into a call.

How to reply

  1. Reply OK in the WhatsApp group if the four texts are good to go.
  2. For a change, send the ad number and your wording, for example: Ad 2, title: …. We apply your words exactly.
  3. After your OK, the visuals come to you for a last look. Nothing goes live without your go.
