Meta ads: the blocker and the plan

Where the ads stand today, what is blocking them, the safe ways to unblock them, and the plan we launch the day they can run.

On hold · blocked by Meta, not by the campaign Ready · a clean Neon Pixels ad account Ready · plan, creatives and tracking checklist

The blocker, in plain words

  1. Meta has limited Marco’s personal Facebook profile because three older ad accounts in his name have unpaid balances.
  2. The Neon Pixels Instagram (@neonpixels.fr) is linked to Marco’s profile. So it can’t be connected to the Neon Pixels business account, whoever tries.
  3. We tried twice, by two different routes. Meta answered “unknown error”, then “Business Account Not Allowed to Advertise”.
  4. Meta’s own assistant confirmed to Marco that this is what limits his profile, and so the Instagram connection.
  5. Everything else is clean: the Neon Pixels business, its ad account (EUR, Paris time) and our own profile show no advertising issues. The Instagram account itself is healthy, with no restrictions.

What it blocks, and what it doesn’t

Blocked today

  • Connecting @neonpixels.fr to the Neon Pixels business account
  • Ads that show as @neonpixels.fr
  • An automatic “DIAGNOSTIC” reply in the Business Suite inbox
  • Possibly any ads from the Neon Pixels ad account. Only Meta can confirm this (option 1 below).

Not blocked

  • The Instagram account itself: a healthy Business account
  • Posting and scheduling: the 15 launch posts are live, and 5 more are scheduled for 3–5 November
  • Preparing the ads, creatives and tracking in advance
  • Google Ads, which Meta’s limits don’t touch

How to unblock it

1

Ask Meta one precise question

Today · free Can the NEON Pixels business run ads from its own ad account, with a new card, while my older ad accounts have unpaid balances?

If the answer is yes, ads can start under the Facebook Page name while the Instagram link waits.

2

Ask Meta how to connect Instagram another way

Same conversation Can another admin of the NEON Pixels business connect @neonpixels.fr to the business, while my profile is limited?

Asked to Meta directly, so nothing is done that Meta could see as getting around its limits. If yes, the ads can run as @neonpixels.fr.

3

Google Ads in the meantime

Optional · separate budget

Not affected by Meta. It reaches people who are already searching for a web studio in Paris, and sends them to the free Diagnostic.

4

Keep Instagram growing

Already running

Three posts a week, so the profile the ads will send people to keeps getting stronger.

Our recommendation: ask Meta questions 1 and 2 now, in one conversation. If Meta says yes to either, we start a small test straight away. If Meta says no to both, we start with Google Ads while Instagram keeps growing.

What we won’t do, and why

  • No new ad accounts, business accounts or Instagram accounts to get around Meta’s limits. Meta treats this as circumvention. It doesn’t fix the Instagram link, and it can get the new accounts and everyone’s profiles restricted: Victor’s, Marco’s and ours.
  • No VPNs and no logins from other countries on the Neon Pixels accounts. They trigger Meta’s security checks.

The ads plan, once they can run

The path for every visitor

1 · New peopleParis and Île-de-France, French speakers, broad targetingAbout €20–30 a dayDiagnostic requests

Creative

  • Made from our posts and reels, so ads and profile look like one brand
  • 9:16 and 4:5, with the hook on screen in the first 2–3 seconds, and subtitles
  • Calm, premium pacing; a few projects shown in depth
  • Logo and the Neon Pulse sound at the end; licensed music only
  • 2–3 hook versions per video

Testing and control

  • 3–5 new ad versions a week. Cut what doesn’t bring requests and move the budget to what does.
  • A stop-loss limit agreed with you
  • Everything is built paused. Nothing spends until you say go, on your own card.
  • Weekly report: spend, requests, cost per request, what changed. Plus a monthly summary.

The budgets are a starting point for a test, to adjust with you. The first one or two weeks cost more per request while Meta learns; real numbers replace every estimate after that.

Tracking and lead handling

  • A pixel (“neon pixels - website”) and a Conversions API connection already exist in the business. First step: check they are installed and working on neonpixels.fr.
  • A “Lead” event and a thank-you page when the Diagnostic form is sent, so Meta can find more people like the ones who request.
  • A « Comment nous avez-vous connus ? » field (“How did you hear about us?”) on the form.
  • A simple lead sheet, and one named person who answers new requests quickly.
